This checkpoint extends executable reciprocal decisions beyond the compiler's
former August 12, 2025 support bound. That bound was not a legal expiration.
Source extraction, effective-time selection, monetary liability and required
reporting lines are checked separately. This is not complete legal coverage or
a qualified production release.

## An actual boundary defect

The prior compiler enumerated some event fields manually and missed the nested
October 5, 2025 transit-entry deadline. An eligible India transit calculation
therefore remained at 10% after the deadline instead of changing to its ordinary
25% reciprocal rate in the isolated regression. The red result is retained.
The compiler now traverses typed legal windows recursively, including claim
deadlines. Date ordering uses absolute instants. The daily interval collector
uses the corresponding window fields; publication and retrieval timestamps
remain evidence metadata. These are generic temporal fixes, not HTS repairs.
The ordinary revision builder now reads the same original-action history as
executable compilation, in addition to product-exemption history. Its country,
partner, sector and claim deadlines therefore reach wide/normalized legal-state
segmentation even when they are absent from that snapshot's product notes.

## Verification and reproduction

`verification.json` and the compressed logs record actual outcomes and limits.
The all-revision census compares original partner provisions with all 55 native
HTS snapshots and independently reconciles the original Annex II history with
each revision's notes. Absent later headings in older snapshots do not mean
legal nonapplicability. The census is source reconciliation, not an exhaustive
shipment-grid oracle. Every typed intra-revision legal boundary is retained,
separately from the human event labels.
An independent R calendar check retains all 22 typed instants within the audit
window across those 55 revisions; 21 fall inside source-revision windows and
one coincides with a revision boundary. This tests interval segmentation, not
an exhaustive numeric duty grid.

The real point-calculator fixtures exercise ordinary normalized/Parquet output,
decision classes and exact-time bundles, then R/TypeScript, DuckDB, JSON, Arrow
and batch delivery. Controlled complete Section 232 attestations in isolated
arithmetic fixtures are explicitly synthetic and never qualify a production
release. All-authority incomplete results retain a NULL complete total.
The semiconductor point fixture additionally tests a charged technical leaf,
its civil-industrial end-use exclusion and an adjacent uncovered product.
Twenty-six point requests are retained with 16 matching legacy MotherDuck
observations. Their contracts differ, so no complete-total delta is asserted.
Node evidence counts individual test cases under Node 22, using direct module
execution rather than treating a successful file wrapper as a test-case count.

Reproduce the source-only census without building any rates:

python3 scripts/audit_reciprocal_policy.py --out /tmp/reciprocal-source-recheck
