## Auto-program dependency

A real EU hose regression initially produced25USD in additional duties on100USD:
12.5USD automobile duty plus12.5USD reciprocal duty. Its General duty is2.5percent.
The country automobile amendment had changed the reporting heading, while the
reciprocal exemption recognized only the original automobile headings.

EO14257 section3(b)(iii) exempts automobiles and parts subject to the additional
duties proclaimed under Proclamation10908. The original country annexes identify
amendments to that programme. The reciprocal decision therefore needs the dated,
source-extracted amended auto headings and actual matched use/claim decisions;
an original suffix list or a generic positive-232 test is insufficient. A zero
non-auto reporting decision must not establish this exception.

The same issue affects UK automobile treatment from June30 and EU treatment from
August1, before the August7 reciprocal country replacement. Those effective
instants must split the executable history independently of HTS revision dates.
Electronics under8471 retain their separate original reciprocal product
exemption even where actual automotive use also incurs Section232.
